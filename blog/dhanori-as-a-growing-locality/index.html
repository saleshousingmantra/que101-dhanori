Dhanori as a growing locality

Dhanori is becoming one of Pune's sought-after residential areas, with schools, hospitals, malls and offices all within reach.

Where Dhanori is

Dhanori is in north-east Pune, next to Lohegaon, Vishrantwadi and Tingre Nagar, and close to Pune Airport. QUE101, also known as Life 360 Dhanori, is on Bharatmata Road, about 5.9 km from the airport.

What has changed

Over the past decade, Dhanori has grown from a quieter edge of the city into a residential area in its own right, with gated communities, schools, restaurants and neighbourhood shopping. Preschools, restaurants and Goodwill Square mall are all within about 2 km of the site, so daily needs rarely mean a long drive.

Unique Properties has already delivered several projects here, including Aeropolis, Silver Mist, Abhilasha and Unique Zest County, which shows its long involvement in the area.

Living here day to day

Work is a manageable trip, with the Viman Nagar, Yerawada and Kharadi IT parks about 8 to 12 km away. Hospitals are within about 6 to 14 km, and Phoenix Marketcity is about 9 km away for a bigger day out.

Like any growing area, some roads are busy at peak times and construction is ongoing in places. Visit at different times of day to get a real feel for the neighbourhood.

Who lives in Dhanori

Dhanori attracts a mix of residents: young professionals working in east Pune's IT parks, families looking for more space than central Pune offers, and long-time Pune residents moving within the city. Its closeness to the airport also suits people who travel often for work.

That mix gives the area a settled, residential feel, with local shops, places of worship, parks and services that serve families day to day.

What to look at before choosing a home here

Visit at different times of day, including weekday mornings and evenings, to see traffic and noise levels. Walk around the immediate surroundings of the project and check how far your everyday needs really are.

Look at the developer's track record in the area. Unique Properties' earlier projects in Dhanori can be visited, which gives you a sense of construction quality and how the communities have aged.

In short

Dhanori has become a settled residential area in north-east Pune, close to the airport and the eastern IT corridor. Schools, restaurants and a mall are within about 2 km of QUE101, IT parks are 8 to 12 km away, and larger hospitals are within about 14 km. Unique Properties has built here before, so you can see its earlier work in person. Visit at different times of day before you decide, and talk to the team about which home size fits your family.
